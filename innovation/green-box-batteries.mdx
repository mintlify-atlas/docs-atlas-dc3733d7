## Company Mission

Green Box Batteries was founded to solve the critical challenges of battery distribution, tracking, and lifecycle management through automated kiosk technology. The company's mission centered on creating a zero-waste, telemetry-enabled system that could track profitability and inventory in real-time while serving enterprise and consumer markets.

## Technical Execution

### Azure IoT Integration

The core technical architecture leveraged **Microsoft Azure IoT** services to enable:

- Real-time telemetry from distributed kiosk units
- Remote monitoring and diagnostic capabilities
- Automated inventory tracking and alerts
- Cloud-based P&L (Profit & Loss) calculation per unit
- Predictive maintenance scheduling

### Hardware Manufacturing Journey

The path from concept to commercial product required mastering multiple domains:

  <Step title="Enterprise Deployment">

  </Step>
