## Data Governance Framework

### Enterprise Governance Principles

<Warning>
Data governance is not optional at enterprise scale. Without robust governance, data quality degrades, compliance risks increase, and the platform becomes a liability rather than an asset.
</Warning>

Established comprehensive data governance framework ensuring quality, security, and compliance:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Data Quality Standards">
    **Quality Dimensions:**
    - **Accuracy**: Data matches real-world truth
    - **Completeness**: Required fields populated
    - **Consistency**: Data aligned across systems
    - **Timeliness**: Data updated in near real-time
    - **Validity**: Data conforms to defined formats
    
    **Enforcement:**
    - Validation rules at point of entry
    - Automated data quality monitoring
    - Regular data quality audits
    - Remediation workflows for issues
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Data Stewardship">
    **Roles & Responsibilities:**
    - **Data Owners**: Business leaders accountable for data domains
    - **Data Stewards**: Subject matter experts ensuring quality
    - **Data Custodians**: Technical teams implementing controls
    - **Data Consumers**: End users with appropriate access
    
    **Governance Processes:**
    - Regular data governance committee meetings
    - Data issue escalation and resolution
    - Policy definition and enforcement
    - Metrics and reporting on governance health
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Privacy & Compliance">
    **Regulatory Requirements:**
    - GDPR data protection and deletion rights
    - CCPA consumer privacy requirements
    - SOC 2 security and availability controls
    - Industry-specific regulations (PCI-DSS, HIPAA)
    
    **Implementation:**
    - Data classification and tagging
    - Encryption at rest and in transit
    - Audit logging and monitoring
    - **100% security compliance adherence** through automation
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Master Data Management

Implemented master data management (MDM) practices to maintain data integrity across systems:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Golden Record Creation">
    Establish single authoritative record for each entity through deduplication and merging
  </Step>
  <Step title="Data Synchronization">
    Propagate golden record updates to downstream systems through integration APIs
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conflict Resolution">
    Define rules for handling conflicting data from multiple sources
  </Step>
  <Step title="Data Lineage">
    Track data origin and transformations for audit and troubleshooting
  </Step>
</Steps>
