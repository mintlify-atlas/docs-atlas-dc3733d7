## Zero Downtime Migration Techniques

### Blue-Green Deployment Strategy

<Info>
Maintained 100% uptime during critical migrations through proven blue-green deployment techniques and comprehensive rollback plans.
</Info>

**Implementation Approach:**

1. **Parallel Environments**: Run old (blue) and new (green) systems in parallel
2. **Gradual Traffic Shift**: Route increasing percentage of traffic to new system
3. **Real-Time Monitoring**: Monitor system health and user experience metrics
4. **Instant Rollback**: Maintain ability to route back to old system if issues arise
5. **Final Cutover**: Complete transition after validation period
